Golden hands,
and they mean it literally.

“Truly they have golden hands.” — that line came from a guest's Google review, not from us. It's the shortest version of what we're trying to do here.

Who we are

A wellness and rejuvenating space in Nairobi.

Golden Hands Spa is a professional spa — private treatment rooms, trained therapists, proper oils and a sauna, run to the same standard at both of our branches.

We started on the 4th floor of the Swiss Lenana Mount Hotel on Ralph Bunche Road in Kilimani, tucked away from the street and next to the gym. It worked, so we opened a second home on Kiambu Road, at High View Plaza in Thindigua — same rooms, same therapists' training, same free sauna with every massage.

Most of our guests find us through Instagram, walk in once, and then come back monthly. That's the entire business model, and it only holds if the treatment is good every single time.

Three words we actually work to

Renew. Refresh. Rejuvenate.
